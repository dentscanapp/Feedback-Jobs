What we do

Two sides, one promise

Whether you're searching for the next step in your career or for the right person to join your team — we make the connection.

For candidates

We help you find a job that truly fits — completely free of charge. We stay with you from the first conversation to your first day at work.

  • Personal job matching to your skills
  • CV review & interview preparation
  • Relocation & paperwork guidance
  • Support after you start the job

For employers

About us

A recruitment partner you can actually reach

Feedback Jobs S.R.L. is a recruitment and job-placement agency based in Sovata, Romania. We bring together candidates and employers across Romania, Hungary, Austria and beyond — building working relationships that last.
